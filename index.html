The product

An AI grading assistant
built around your course and your judgment.

Your LMS stores courses and clicks. Edutecnik helps with the academic work around them: grading with your rubric, writing useful feedback, preparing class, and spotting recurring gaps.

Grade with your rubric

Upload a PDF, image, or pasted assignment. Edutecnik proposes a score and comments against your own rubric; you edit every one before it counts.

  • Your rubric, not a generic grading scheme
  • Draft feedback tied to specific criteria
  • No grade changes without your approval

Prepare class faster

Draft lesson plans, activities, and assessments from your own syllabus, PDFs, learning objectives, and course materials.

  • Aligned to your course outcomes
  • Grounded in material you uploaded
  • Copy or export wherever you teach

See patterns across the course

Turn the work you already grade into a clearer view of recurring misconceptions, weak rubric criteria, and what may need attention next.

  • Course-level patterns from real grading
  • Human review before consequential action
  • Useful with or without an LMS

How it works

Four steps, with a human decision at the end of each.

  1. 01

    Import

    Bring your syllabus, PDFs, rubrics, assignments, and student work.

  2. 02

    Analyze and draft

    Edutecnik drafts grades, rubric-based feedback, lesson plans, assessments, and course patterns.

  3. 03

    Review and approve

    You edit, approve, or discard. No consequential decision is made without you.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Copy or export what you approved. LMS integrations can later write approved work back to the systems you already use.

Trust

AI assistance without giving away academic judgment.

Human approval

No consequential action happens without a person approving it. Edutecnik does not independently change a grade or contact a student.

Rubric alignment

Grading suggestions are built around the instructor's criteria rather than a generic grading standard.

Transparency

Drafts are labeled as drafts, and instructors can review the basis for suggestions.

Traceability

Suggestions, edits, and decisions can be tracked so academic review has real evidence.

Student privacy

The product is designed to minimize exposure of identifying student information when work is processed.

Role-based access

Instructor, chair, curriculum, and administration roles can be governed according to institutional responsibilities.
